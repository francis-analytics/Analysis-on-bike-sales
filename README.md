# Analysis-on-bike-sales

This project focuses on analyzing bike sales data to understand customer behavior, purchasing patterns, and key factors that influence bike buying decisions. The analysis provides actionable insights into demographics, income levels, and commuting habits to support better marketing and sales strategies.
The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that can guide business decisions and improve targeting of potential customers.
🎯 Objectives
Understand customer demographics influencing bike purchases
Analyze income levels and their relationship with buying decisions
Identify commuting patterns of bike buyers vs non-buyers
Explore key factors that drive sales performance
Provide insights to improve marketing targeting strategies
🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Excel 
Data Cleaning & Preparation
Data Visualization

📊 Key Analysis Areas
Customer age distribution
Income vs bike purchase behavior
Commute distance impact on bike purchases
Gender-based purchasing patterns
Regional or category-based trends

📈 Visualizations Included
Bar charts for income and purchase comparison
Pie charts for demographic breakdown
Interactive dashboards

💡 Key Insights
Identified clear relationship between income level and bike purchasing behavior
Found that commuting distance significantly influences bike purchases
Highlighted demographic groups with higher likelihood of buying bikes
Revealed patterns useful for targeted marketing campaigns
